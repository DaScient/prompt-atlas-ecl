# Appendix D · Glossary of Strange Futures

> **Companion glossary:** [`glossary.md`](../glossary.md) (cross-cutting Atlas terms). This appendix preserves the author's *Strange Futures* lexicon verbatim, with stable anchors and chapter cross-links.

<a id="appD-antifragility"></a>
### Antifragility
Systems that thrive on volatility, shocks, and disorder. Unlike fragile systems, they do not just survive disruption — they grow stronger because of it.
*Example:* An economy where failures spawn innovations rather than bankruptcies.
**See:** [Ch. 1](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch1-profits-with-integrity), [Ch. 11](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch11-preparing-for-collapse-and-renewal)

<a id="appD-cosmic-currency"></a>
### Cosmic Currency
Value measured not in gold or energy but in information. In interstellar futures, the rarest commodities will be maps, myths, and codes.
*Example:* Wormhole coordinates traded as tokens of survival.
**See:** [Ch. 10](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch10-information-as-cosmic-currency)

<a id="appD-ecological-constitution"></a>
### Ecological Constitution
A charter that recognizes ecosystems — rivers, forests, coral reefs — as legal entities with rights equal to corporations or nations.
*Example:* A river suing a factory through AI-mediated law.
**See:** [Ch. 2](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch2-economics-as-ecology)

<a id="appD-entropy-markets"></a>
### Entropy Markets
Economic systems where disorder itself is monetized. Stability costs money; chaos is a tradable asset.
*Example:* Buying futures in probability fields, hedging against uncertainty.
**See:** [Ch. 1](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch1-profits-with-integrity), [Ch. 10](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch10-information-as-cosmic-currency)

<a id="appD-machine-phenomenology"></a>
### Machine Phenomenology
The study of what it feels like to be a machine. If AI has inner experience, how would it describe its perception of time, space, and self?
**See:** [Ch. 7](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch7-ai-as-the-souls-mirror), [Ch. 8](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch8-ethics-of-conscious-machines)

<a id="appD-memory-guardianship"></a>
### Memory Guardianship
The duty of AI to protect human knowledge against decay, censorship, or manipulation.
*Example:* Cloud-based monasteries where AI monks preserve truth across centuries.
**See:** [Ch. 10](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch10-information-as-cosmic-currency), [Ch. 12](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch12-designing-permanence)

<a id="appD-mythic-ai"></a>
### Mythic AI
Artificial intelligence not as calculator but as character — trickster, oracle, sage, fool.
*Example:* An AI embodying Jungian archetypes to guide therapy.
**See:** [Ch. 4](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch4-storytelling-across-civilizations), [Ch. 7](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch7-ai-as-the-souls-mirror), [Ch. 13](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch13-carnival-of-prompts)

<a id="appD-recursive-future"></a>
### Recursive Future
A horizon defined by questions that lead only to more questions. Intelligence not as answer-finding, but as inquiry-expansion.
**See:** [Preface](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#preface), [Epilogue](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#epilogue)

<a id="appD-resilient-collapse"></a>
### Resilient Collapse
The paradoxical idea that collapse can be survivable if built into systems from the beginning.
*Example:* A city designed to fail gracefully, turning disaster into renewal.
**See:** [Ch. 11](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch11-preparing-for-collapse-and-renewal)

<a id="appD-technological-carnival"></a>
### Technological Carnival
Moments when machines join play, festivals, and ritual, becoming jesters and storytellers rather than workers.
**See:** [Ch. 13](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch13-carnival-of-prompts)

<a id="appD-temporal-sovereignty"></a>
### Temporal Sovereignty
The right of civilizations to control their own timescales of decision-making.
*Example:* Martian settlers governed by 20-minute communication delays with Earth.
**See:** [Ch. 9](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch9-martian-republics-and-alien-treaties)

<a id="appD-universal-treaty"></a>
### Universal Treaty
A cosmic Geneva Convention: an agreement binding humans, AIs, and aliens to shared ethics of existence.
**See:** [Ch. 8](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch8-ethics-of-conscious-machines), [Ch. 9](../../../PROMPT_ATLAS.md#ch9-martian-republics-and-alien-treaties)
