# Appendix E · Reading & Resource Atlas

> A curated guide for thinkers, creators, and leaders who want to explore further. Reproduces the author's lists with light editorial polish and a suggested reading pathway.

## Foundational Texts

- **Plato** — *The Republic* — questions of governance, myth, and justice.
- **Richard Feynman** — *The Pleasure of Finding Things Out* — curiosity as method.
- **Elinor Ostrom** — *Governing the Commons* — models for cooperative survival.
- **Ursula K. Le Guin** — *The Dispossessed* — anarchist futures and moral imagination.
- **Bruno Latour** — *We Have Never Been Modern* — rethinking human/non-human divides.

## Scientific Frontiers

- **NASA Open Data Portal** — `https://data.nasa.gov` — planetary exploration datasets.
- **CERN Open Science** — `https://opendata.cern.ch` — particle physics data archives.
- **Human Genome Project** — historical and contemporary genomic foundations.
- **IPCC Reports** — `https://www.ipcc.ch` — climate science at planetary scale.

## Cultural & Mythic Resources

- **The Internet Archive** — `https://archive.org` — billions of digitized cultural artifacts.
- **Project Gutenberg** — `https://www.gutenberg.org` — free library of human literature.
- **The World Oral Literature Project** — `https://www.oralliterature.org` — preserving endangered myths.
- **Long Now Foundation** — `https://longnow.org` — projects like the 10,000-year clock.

## Communities & Institutions

- **Future of Humanity Institute (Oxford)** — existential risks and ethics.
- **MIT Media Lab** — `https://www.media.mit.edu` — interdisciplinary futures of technology and culture.
- **Santa Fe Institute** — `https://www.santafe.edu` — complexity science and adaptive systems.
- **The Long Now Seminars** — `https://longnow.org/seminars/` — conversations on deep time.

## Digital Toolkits

- **Kaggle** — `https://www.kaggle.com` — community datasets and AI challenges.
- **Our World in Data** — `https://ourworldindata.org` — visualizations of global issues.
- **Metaculus** — `https://www.metaculus.com` — prediction markets for future events.
- **Hugging Face** — `https://huggingface.co` — open-source AI research and tools.

## Suggested Reading Pathway

1. **Start with Awe** — Feynman, Le Guin, Latour.
2. **Move into Systems** — Ostrom, Santa Fe Institute resources.
3. **Expand to Cosmos** — NASA, CERN, SETI.
4. **Return to Culture** — Long Now, Internet Archive, myth projects.

## Guide for AI & Humanity

- **Read across canons.** A reading list dominated by one tradition will produce a mind dominated by one tradition.
- **Slow reading is strategic.** Treat at least one resource per quarter as something to sit with for months, not skim.
- **Cite generously.** Provenance is a form of solidarity.
