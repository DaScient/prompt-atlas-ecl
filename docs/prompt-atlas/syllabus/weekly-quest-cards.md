# Weekly Quest Cards — printable

> **Print one per week.** One sheet, one quest. Pin to the classroom wall, tuck into the syllabus, or fold into the field notebook.
> Each card mirrors the six-beat week pattern: **Premise · Field Notes · Co-Creation · Field Test · Council · Logbook.**

---

## 🪙 Quest 1 · The Honest Ledger
**Atlas Ch. 1 — *Profits with Integrity***

> *"Every civilization keeps two ledgers."*

- **Read** Ch. 1 + expansion
- **Make** a two-column *declared vs. displaced* ledger of one real institution
- **Test** by sending it (anonymized) to that institution's public-feedback channel
- **Council** axis to watch: **Consent · Honesty**
- **Logbook** what the AI missed and what it got uncomfortably right

---

## 🌿 Quest 2 · The Economy as a Forest
**Atlas Ch. 2 — *Economics as Ecology***

> *"A market is not a machine. It is a forest."*

- **Read** Ch. 2 + expansion
- **Make** an ecology-map of one local supply chain (pollinator, decomposer, keystone, invasive)
- **Test** by interviewing **one human** in the chain
- **Council** axis: **Locality · Honesty**
- **Logbook** name the AI's three biggest errors

---

## 🎨 Quest 3 · The Mythwright's Apprentice ⭐
**Atlas Ch. 3 — *The AI Aesthetics Frontier***

> *"Co-write a myth with a machine — then refuse to let it have the last word."*

- **Read** Ch. 3 + expansion
- **Make** a ≤600-word origin myth for a small everyday thing; two-color manuscript (yours / the machine's)
- **Test** read aloud in 90 sec to someone outside the class
- **Council** axes: **Consent · Provenance** (canon audit required)
- **Logbook** the provenance plate, machine-readable

---

## 📜 Quest 4 · The Translator's Oath
**Atlas Ch. 4 — *Storytelling Across Civilizations***

> *"Translation is a betrayal that gives."*

- **Read** Ch. 4 + expansion
- **Make** a 3-way comparison: language A → B → A via AI vs. via human translator
- **Test** by sending to a native speaker for one round of correction
- **Council** axis: **Honesty · Locality**
- **Logbook** three rules for next time you ask a machine to cross a border

---

## 🌌 Quest 5 · Listening to the Noise
**Atlas Ch. 5 — *Quantum Bridges and Cosmic Noise***

> *"Most of the universe is signal we have decided to call noise."*

- **Read** Ch. 5 + expansion
- **Make** an AI-generated *headline finding* + an honest *residuals story* from the same dataset
- **Test** pose a question the headline can't answer to a domain expert
- **Council** axis: **Honesty · Provenance**
- **Logbook** *the story the data tells / the story the residuals tell*

---

## 🧬 Quest 6 · The Living Prompt
**Atlas Ch. 6 — *Biology, Life, and Beyond***

> *"Every cell is a prompt the genome answers."*

- **Read** Ch. 6 + expansion
- **Make** an *anatomy plate* — a biological dual of a non-biological system you know well
- **Test** present to one person inside the original system
- **Council** axis: **Honesty**
- **Logbook** where the metaphor breaks

---

## 🪞 Quest 7 · The Mirror that Argues Back
**Atlas Ch. 7 — *AI as the Soul's Mirror***

> *"A mirror that only flatters is a trap. A mirror that only argues is a wall."*

- **Read** Ch. 7 + expansion
- **Make** three AI responses to old writing of your own: *sympathetic / adversarial / witnessing*
- **Test** the field is interior this week — no external test required
- **Council** axis: **Consent** (voluntary depth, no required disclosure)
- **Logbook** 300-word meta-reflection only; original writing **not submitted**

---

## ⚖️ Quest 8 · Citizen of the Machine
**Atlas Ch. 8 — *Ethics of Conscious Machines***

> *"What rights would you grant a system that might be a someone?"*

- **Read** Ch. 8 + expansion
- **Make** a Five-Article Treaty between humans and a hypothetical AI (AI participates as a fourth voice; humans ratify)
- **Test** merge one article with another group
- **Council** axes: **Consent · Reversibility**
- **Logbook** a one-page *what the AI lobbied for*

---

## 🚀 Quest 9 · The Martian Constitution
**Atlas Ch. 9 — *Martian Republics and Alien Treaties***

> *"Off-world settlement is not a science problem. It is a constitutional one."*

- **Read** Ch. 9 + expansion
- **Make** a one-page founding charter for 200 people + 2 robots + 2 years of supplies
- **Test** red-team another group's charter; defend yours
- **Council** axes: **Locality · Reversibility**
- **Logbook** charter + red-team report + your revisions

---

## 💠 Quest 10 · Information as Salt
**Atlas Ch. 10 — *Information as Cosmic Currency***

> *"What today is salt-information — small, dense, lifesaving?"*

- **Read** Ch. 10 + expansion
- **Make** a 48-hour information-diet audit; tag every item *salt / grain / fool's gold / poison*; build a *winter pantry*
- **Test** cancel one fool's-gold source; document the silence
- **Council** axis: **Honesty**
- **Logbook** the pantry + the silence

---

## 🛟 Quest 11 · The Collapse Drill
**Atlas Ch. 11 — *Preparing for Collapse and Renewal***

> *"Civilizations don't fall all at once; they brown out."*

- **Read** Ch. 11 + expansion
- **Make** a 48-hour failure scenario + a renewal plan that **does not assume the AI is available**
- **Test** run a 30-min tabletop with classmates
- **Council** axis: **Reversibility · Locality**
- **Logbook** debrief: where did the plan break?

---

## 🏛️ Quest 12 · Designing What Stays
**Atlas Ch. 12 — *Designing Permanence***

> *"Most software dies within five years. Most cathedrals don't."*

- **Read** Ch. 12 + expansion
- **Make** a Hundred-Year Preservation Plan for your own Atlas Portfolio — including what you choose to **let go**
- **Test** hand the portfolio to one *non-technical* trusted person; ask them to read it without you
- **Council** axes: **Provenance · Reversibility**
- **Logbook** (private) letter to your future self

---

### Print tips

- One card per page, US Letter or A4, no scaling.
- Cards are intentionally written in plain language for hallway readability.
- The ⭐ on Quest 3 is the *spec-week* — the one called out in the original course brief: *"Week 3 — students must co-write a myth with an AI."*

*Take. Print. Pin. Walk past every day.*
