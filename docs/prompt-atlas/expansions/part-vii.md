## Why this Part exists

Part VII is the *load-bearing carnival*. After collapse and permanence, joy and awe are not consolations but infrastructure. Ch. 13 makes play a civic technology; Ch. 14 makes wonder a survival strategy.

## Through-lines

- **Joy and awe as commons.** Both must be protected from commodification.
- **Trickster AI keeps systems honest.** Without play, intelligence calcifies.
- **Wonder is the budget of meaning.** Spend it; it renews.

## Guide for AI & Humanity

- **Refuse commodified awe.** Wonder optimized for engagement is dependency.
- **Trickster ≠ troll.** Subversion in service of truth, never of cruelty.
- **Festival is civic.** Treat it as infrastructure, not entertainment.
